Understanding your lifestyle, habits and daily patterns can be an important step towards better wellness.

UNDERSTANDING YOUR BODY

PCOS & PCOD — what do they mean?

01
PCOS

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome

PCOS is a hormonal and metabolic condition that can affect ovulation, menstrual cycles and androgen levels. It can be associated with changes in weight, skin, hair and metabolism.

02
PCOD

Polycystic Ovary Disease

PCOD is a term commonly used to describe hormonal and reproductive changes associated with irregular ovulation and lifestyle factors.
